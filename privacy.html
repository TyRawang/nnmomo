Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Nom Nom Momo is a Tibetan family-run catering business based in Oakton, Virginia. This policy explains what information we receive when you use this website or contact us, and how we use it. The short version: this website doesn’t collect your details. You only share information with us when you choose to email us.

The short version

  • We don’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • Our request forms don’t send anything to us. They prepare an email in your own email app, and nothing reaches us until you press send.
  • We use the details you email us only to answer your inquiry and plan your catering.
  • We don’t sell or rent your information.

Information we receive

We receive information only when you contact us, usually by email. This can include:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Event details such as the date, location, guest count and the menu you’re interested in.
  • Dietary needs or allergy information you choose to share so we can discuss what we can accommodate.
  • Anything else you include in your message, and details needed to arrange pickup, delivery and payment.

How our request forms work

When you fill in a request form on this website and press “Prepare my request”, your browser builds an email draft using the details you typed. The form runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored on our website or sent to us at this stage.

You can then review the draft in your email app and decide whether to send it, or copy the text instead. Your details reach us only when you send that email.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry, check availability and send you a quote.
  • To plan, prepare and deliver or hand over your order.
  • To take dietary requests into account when we discuss your menu.
  • To keep a record of orders and correspondence for our business and tax records.
  • To contact you about your order. We won’t add you to a marketing list unless you ask us to.

When we share information

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only when needed to run our business, for example:

  • With our email provider, which stores our messages.
  • With a payment provider when you pay for an order. We plan to offer online ordering through Square; when it’s available, Square will process those payments under its own privacy policy.
  • If we are required to by law.

Third-party services on this website

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google’s servers, which means Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. You can read how Google handles this in the Google Privacy Policy.

Like most websites, the service that hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

Cookies

This website doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t use analytics or advertising tools. If that changes, we’ll update this policy first.

How long we keep information

We keep emails and order details for as long as we need them to handle your inquiry, fulfill your order, and meet our record-keeping and tax obligations. After that, we delete information we no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it when we no longer need to keep it. Email us at nomnommomocatering@gmail.com and we’ll help.

Children

Our services are meant for adults planning events. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email nomnommomocatering@gmail.com.
