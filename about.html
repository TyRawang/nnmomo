Our story

Momos are the dumplings Tibetan families make together. Someone rolls the dough, someone mixes the filling, and everyone folds. By the time the steamer is ready, the kitchen is full of people and stories.

Nom Nom Momo grew out of that feeling. Our name is playful, but our focus is simple: Tibetan momos for the gatherings that matter to you. A team lunch. A birthday. A table full of people you’re happy to see.

We’re a small family business, and we cook for each order. That means every platter, lunch box and gathering spread is planned with you, ahead of time, so it arrives the way we’d serve it at home.

The flavors of home

Tibetan cooking grows from the high plateau: hardy grains, garden peas, wild herbs and mountain flowers. These are the plants that shape the kitchen we come from.

  • Qingko barley
  • Wheat
  • Common buckwheat
  • Peas
  • Tibetan sea-buckthorn
  • Highland rhododendron
