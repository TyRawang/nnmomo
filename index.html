A few good questions

How many momos should I order?+

As a starting point, plan 4–5 per person as an appetizer, or 8–10 as a meal. Dumpling size, appetite, sides and other food all matter. We’ll help you confirm the right quantities.

Is there a minimum order?+

Tray catering starts at a $100 food order. Lunch boxes have a minimum of 10 boxes in total. Your request is reviewed before any order is confirmed.

Can you help with dietary needs?+

We offer a vegetable momo option. Tell us about dietary needs and allergies in your inquiry so we can discuss ingredients and what we can accommodate. We don’t make allergen-free guarantees.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?+

We’re based in Oakton, Virginia. Pickup or delivery availability, timing and any delivery fee are confirmed for your event. Please arrange everything with us before visiting.

How far ahead should I reach out?+

Aim for at least 48 hours for standard catering requests and 72 hours for 200 or more momos. Availability depends on our schedule and the size of your gathering.
