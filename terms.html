Using this website

This website lets you browse our menu and prepare a catering request. You can’t pay for or complete an order on this website. Request forms prepare an email in your own email app; nothing is sent to us until you send that email.

We try to keep menu items, prices and descriptions accurate and up to date, but mistakes can happen. If something on the website is wrong, the details in your confirmed quote apply.

Requests and confirmation

A catering request is not a booking. Your order is confirmed only when we reply by email to confirm availability, the menu, quantities, the final price and the pickup or delivery arrangements.

  • We review every request and may decline one we can’t fulfill.
  • Please check the details in our confirmation and let us know right away if anything is wrong.

Notice and minimums

  • Standard catering requests need at least 48 hours’ notice.
  • Orders of 200 or more momos need at least 72 hours’ notice.
  • Tray catering starts at a $100 food order.
  • Office lunch boxes have a minimum of 10 boxes in total.

Availability depends on our schedule and the size of your order, so earlier requests are always easier for us to accommodate.

Prices and payment

Menu prices on this website are shown before tax and any delivery fee. Your quote will show the final price, including any tax, delivery fee or other charges agreed for your event.

Payment terms, including when payment is due and how to pay, are set out in your quote. We plan to offer online ordering and payment through Square; until then, we’ll arrange payment with you directly.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel an order, please email us as soon as possible. We’ll do our best to accommodate changes, but we may not be able to change or refund an order once we’ve bought ingredients or started preparing it. Any change, cancellation or refund terms for your event will be confirmed in your quote or by email.

If we have to cancel an order for reasons outside our control, such as illness, severe weather or an emergency, we’ll tell you as soon as possible and refund any payment you’ve made for that order.

Pickup and delivery

We’re catering only, with no walk-in service. Pickup or delivery availability, timing and any delivery fee are agreed for each event. Please arrange everything with us before visiting.

For deliveries, please make sure someone is available to receive the order at the agreed time and place. Equipment and staffing are not included unless agreed in your quote.

Food safety and allergies

Please tell us about any dietary needs and allergies when you request catering, so we can discuss ingredients and what we can accommodate. We don’t make allergen-free guarantees, and we can’t rule out cross-contact.

Food should be kept at a safe temperature and served promptly. Once an order has been picked up or delivered, the customer is responsible for storing, reheating and serving it safely. We recommend eating catered food within two hours of pickup or delivery, and refrigerating leftovers promptly.

Food images on this website are illustrative. Final recipes, portions, presentation and packaging may vary and are confirmed with your catering request.
